# ScreenBuddy

ScreenBuddy is a Telegram assistant that recommends movies and shows based on your mood, preferences, and what you want to feel.

## Main Goal

ScreenBuddy's goal is to understand you through a natural conversation and quickly recommend something you genuinely want to watch.

## How It Finds Your Match

Tell ScreenBuddy how your day was, what mood you are in, or what kind of viewing experience you want. It may ask a few short questions to better understand your taste, energy, and preferences before suggesting movies or shows that fit you.

If the recommendations do not feel right, explain what you want changed. ScreenBuddy will use your feedback to find a better match.

## How to Start

1. Open the ScreenBuddy bot on Telegram.
2. Send `/start`.
3. Describe your mood, your day, or what you feel like watching.
4. Answer ScreenBuddy's short follow-up questions.
5. Review the recommendations and share feedback to improve the matches.

## Telegram Commands

- `/start` starts ScreenBuddy and begins a new recommendation conversation.
- `/new` clears the current conversation and starts fresh.
- `/help` explains how to use ScreenBuddy.
